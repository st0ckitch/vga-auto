About Us

VG Auto Export is an international shipping company that turns importing a car from the leading US auctions into one seamless, well-organized and stress-free process. We know how much every detail matters, which is why we offer our customers a complete logistics chain - from the first consultation to handing the car over to its owner.

Our goal is to bring world-class service to Georgia - service built on complete transparency, speed, cargo safety and customer peace of mind. With VG Auto Export, buying a car is simple, safe and comfortable.

  • 01Purchase and Selection. Help choosing the best option and bidding at the auction.
  • 02Secure Shipping. Reliable logistics from US ports to the port of Poti or Batumi.
  • 03Door-to-Door Delivery. Full support and transportation of your car to the address of your choice.
