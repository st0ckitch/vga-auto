Hybrid, Gasoline or Electric - Which Should You Import from the US

10.05.2026

American car auctions do not limit your choice - gasoline, diesel, hybrid or electric, there is something for every budget and need. But which type should you settle on, and what financial and logistical considerations should you keep in mind?

1. Hybrid and Plug-in Hybrid

  • Advantages: high fuel economy and efficiency in city and stop-and-go traffic.
  • Tax break: in Georgia, hybrid cars enjoy a 60% break on excise (customs) duty, which makes importing them from auction even more financially rewarding.
  • What to look for: when inspecting a lot, it is important to assess the condition of the hybrid system and the high-voltage battery.

2. Gasoline

  • Advantages: the classic and most common choice. Known for simple operation and relatively inexpensive maintenance.
  • Parts availability: the spare parts market is the widest - original parts for American models are easy to find (including in our auto parts section).
  • Customs duty: taxed at the standard rate according to engine displacement and year of manufacture.

3. Electric Vehicle (EV)

  • Advantages: 0% customs duty (fully exempt from excise tax), minimal day-to-day running costs and quick acceleration.
  • What to look for: before buying, it is essential to check the battery health index (SOH - State of Health), as well as to assess the charging infrastructure in the area where you live.

4. Diesel

  • Advantages: high torque and fuel economy over long distances and on the highway.
  • Specifics: at American auctions, diesel engines are found mainly in pickup trucks and large SUVs.

How to make the right decision

The final choice depends on your daily needs and the routes you drive:

  • Calculating your full budget: use the shipping calculator on our website, and check the exact customs rate on RS.ge.
  • Free consultation: request a call and the VG Auto Export team will help you choose the optimal car for your budget.
