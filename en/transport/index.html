Service

How We Ship Your Vehicle

We handle the entire journey of your vehicle from the USA to Georgia: inland transport from the auction to a US port, followed by container ocean freight to Poti or Batumi. Inside the container, every vehicle is secured in line with safety standards.

For full transparency, you receive a photo report at every stage - from auction pickup to container loading and arrival at the port. You always know exactly where your car is and what condition it is in.

Auction Pickup

The vehicle is carried overland from the auction to the warehouse at the nearest port.

Container Loading

Once received at the warehouse, the vehicle is loaded into a container - and you get a photo report.

Ocean Freight

The container sails to Georgia aboard a container ship - to the port of Poti or Batumi.

Arrival in Georgia

After arriving at the port, the vehicle clears customs and is prepared for handover to its owner.
