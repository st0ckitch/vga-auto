Process

How It Works

Four stages — from choosing a car to handover at the port of Poti.

  1. Choose and Buy

    Pick a car at a US auto auction. We will help you with the selection and the purchase.

  2. Documentation

    Export documents are prepared according to the requirements of the relevant state.

  3. Shipping

    The vehicle travels from the auction to a US port, then continues by container across the sea.

  4. Handover in Poti

    The car arrives at the port of Poti. A 4-stage photo report accompanies the entire process.
