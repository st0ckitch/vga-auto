How We Work

What the Car Import Service Includes

Importing a car from the USA takes several stages - from selection at auction to handover at the port of Poti. VG Auto Export handles every stage in-house, while you follow the process online by VIN.

We work with vehicles from Copart, IAAI, and other major US auto auctions. Before buying, we recommend checking our export documentation table - not every state's paperwork is valid for export.

  • Selection at auction
  • Purchase and payment
  • Inland transport in the USA
  • Container shipping
  • Customs clearance
  • Handover in Poti

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the import process take?

The timeline depends on the state, the auction location, and the shipping line's schedule. Every stage of the process - auction, transport, port, Poti - is confirmed with a photo report and can be tracked online via VIN search.

How much does importing a car cost?

The price depends on the vehicle type, the auction location, and the port. For an estimate, use the or contact us.

Which auctions can I buy from?

We work with Copart, IAAI, Manheim, and other major US auto auctions. To learn how auction bidding works, see our video tutorials.

How can I be sure a vehicle's documents are valid for export?

Check the state in our export documentation table - some states' documents (e.g. certain California title types) are not suitable for export. Always verify with us before buying.

Can I follow the process?

Yes - a photo report follows your vehicle through all four stages: the auction, transport in the USA, the US port, and Poti. For the shipping line's transit status, use .
